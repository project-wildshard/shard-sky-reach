# The flock is declared rows (meta ONE-FORMAT OF20b)

> Written by: Claude (opus-of20-sky, from opus-of20 and opus-of-close2's drafts)

- The goats, drift rays, roost rays and gale wisps leave the runtime plan's keeper: `speciesProgramToRows` turns their
  species into rows, and each is a declared spawn with its keeper id, home and scale (`rows/declaredCreatures.ts`). The
  keeper keeps only the Storm Roc until OF20c. The page's groups still name the flock (the ray wake, the witness).
- Engine 0.1.53 (release `b889e7396`) carries the engine half (`b96ed42f7`: declared bodies take the plan's solids floor
  and `killY`, page groups may name declared spawns) and format 0.54.0 is the one it pins. SDK stays 0.4.17 here;
  0.4.18 follows with OF20c.
- `test/declared-population.test.ts` stands the built product's cell: goats keep their isles, a goat shoved off the rim
  dies under the death plane, a ray dives, the cell saves and resumes.
- The walk checkpoint for 0.1.53 on darwin-arm64 was recorded on a pack of release/v0.1.53; linux-x64 follows from CI.
