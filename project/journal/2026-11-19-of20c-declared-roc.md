> Written by: Claude (opus-of20-sky, from opus-of-close2's drafts)

- The Roc joins the declared flock with no brain or blow of its own: it rests on its perch and its decision row
  (`ROC_DECISION`, run by the declared decision host by id) moves and strikes it. The keeper stands nothing.
- Its fight is the declared `boss` encounter `far.roc.fight` (`resident`, `capExempt`) with its `bossPanel` ui row. The
  plan keeps ROC_OWNER, so the win still sets `far.roc.down`, files `far-reach.roc` and pays the purse once. The page
  drops its `boss.held` feature; the shard file's `spawn` is the manifest's.
- A plain strike row stops at 20 m, so the Roc's plain rows clamp; its exact stoop and gale wall stay in
  `actors/strikes`, which the decision host runs over the plain row by the same id. The ability rows carry the gale
  wall's push and the stoop's chest aim (format 0.49.0, engine `7eafc59f1`).
- Needs engine 0.1.53 (`3802b5bce4`: a plan owner row on a declared boss needs no page boss feature).
- The boss test failed first on OF20b's tree (no encounters) and passes here: 64 vitest cases, both journeys,
  product-play. The darwin walk checkpoint is re-recorded for the moved Roc.
